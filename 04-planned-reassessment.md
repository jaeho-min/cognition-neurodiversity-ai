## Planned reassessment 
 
A current adult ADHD and cognitive reassessment is being prepared. 
 
The planned assessment includes or is expected to include: 
  
- K-WAIS-IV  
- CAT attention testing  
- DIVA-5  
- Barkley-based ADHD / executive-function assessment  
- additional executive-function or functional-impairment measures where appropriate 
 
The purpose is not simply to subtract a childhood IQ score from an adult IQ score. 
 
The more useful comparison is likely to involve the structure of the profile: 
 
- attention  
- response consistency  
- working memory  
- processing speed  
- executive regulation  
- functional impairment  
- relative strengths and weaknesses 
 
 
## The central question 
 
At this stage, I have: 
 
- a hospital-certified childhood clinical record  
- a historical ADHD assessment  
- historical cognitive and attention findings  
- years of AI interaction  
- a reported change in how I structure problems  
- and dated real-world artifacts showing changes in what I build, test and document 
 
What I do not yet have is evidence establishing the causal relationship between them. 
 
That is the point of this project. 
 
> Can cognitive bottlenecks be identified, externalized and systematically reduced without outsourcing the thinking itself?
